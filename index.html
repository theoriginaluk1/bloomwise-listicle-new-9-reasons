🧠 Four, not one

Focus Isn't One Switch. It's Four.

Here's what nobody hands you at the doctor's office, and it explains both sides. Why the meds work at school, and why the stories sound the way they do.

Your child's focus, moods, sleep, and self-control don't run on one brain chemical. They run on four:

  • Dopamine. The focus one.
  • Serotonin. Moods and sleep.
  • GABA. The brakes. It helps them stop before acting.

Stimulant medicines push ONE of those, hard. Dopamine. The other three get nothing.

So focus goes up, and everything else stays hard. The crash. The sleep. The appetite. That's not a mystery. That's three parts of the brain going without help while one gets pushed into overdrive.
